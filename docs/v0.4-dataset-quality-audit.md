## 1. Audit Scope & Methodology

This quality audit inspects the generated OLORIC v0.4 dataset across both automated schema constraints and deep qualitative pedagogical dimensions. Sampling adheres to the mandated audit specifications:
1. **At least 2 examples from every canonical category** (20 categories × ≥2 = 40+ audited).
2. **At least 2 examples from every domain** (6 domains × ≥2 = 12+ audited).
3. **All 24 prior-error examples** (100% census of Capability 1).
4. **All five document-grounding behavior types** (Behaviors A, B, C-Pattern A/B, D, E).
5. **Every benchmark concept** (all 18 unique concepts across the 20 benchmark records).
6. **At least 5 multi-turn examples** (audited across diverse categories).

Every sampled record was evaluated across its complete payload:
- `instruction`: Clarity, task alignment, and absence of template artifacts.
- `task`: Consistency with category and learner objective.
- `conversation_context`: Naturalness, conversational coherence, and role fidelity.
- `document_context`: Authentic academic context, genuine citations, realistic excerpts.
- `target.response`: Pedagogical rigor, clarity, accuracy, and tone.
- `strategy`: Exact alignment with taxonomy.
- `diagnosis`: Accurate confusion classification, realistic severity, coherent addressed misconception.
- `understanding_check`: Diagnostic validity, expected answer fidelity.
- `memory`: Strict conformity to memory hygiene invariants (`candidate=False` fields nullified).
