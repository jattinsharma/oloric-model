# Oloric v0.3 — Root-Cause Audit of v0.2 Behavioral Failures

**Date**: September 26, 2026  
**Subject**: Exhaustive root-cause audit of the four major behavioral failure modes identified in Oloric v0.2 post-training semantic evaluation (`evaluation/oloric-v0.2/semantic_report.md` and `evaluation/oloric-v0.2/failure_cases.md`).  
**Status**: Read-only engineering audit. All v0.2 artifacts, splits, checkpoints, benchmarks, and evaluation records remain frozen and unmodified.  
**Scope**: `data/generated/oloric_v02_dataset.jsonl` (480 records), `data/splits_v02/` (384 train / 48 val / 48 test), `evaluation/benchmark.jsonl` (20 records), `evaluation/oloric-v0.2/responses.jsonl`, `scripts/fix_seed_data_v02.py`, `scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py`, `src/oloric/formatting.py`, `src/oloric/evaluation.py`, and `src/oloric/training.py`.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Oloric v0.2 successfully resolved the fatal template collapse of v0.1: all 20 benchmark responses are schema-valid JSON, zero bracket placeholders (`[concrete example]`, `[hint 1]`) remain, and average factual correctness improved from 0.05 to 2.20. However, structured semantic evaluation exposed four critical behavioral failures:

1. **Task-Type Following Collapse**: 13 of 20 benchmark responses defaulted to `strategy = "concrete_example"` (and 16/20 generated concrete or numerical examples in the response body), completely ignoring requested pedagogical actions (`diagnostic_questions`, `follow_up_questions`, `practice_questions`, `simple_explanation`).
2. **Cross-Domain & Concept-Bleed Example Contamination**: Fixed numerical and concrete examples (such as a 2000-calorie diet formula or water synthesis stoichiometry) appeared repeatedly across unrelated concepts and domains (e.g., calorie formulas applied to Enzyme Activity, Food Preservation, Nutrient Deficiencies, and Dietary Fiber; water stoichiometry applied to Cell Mitosis and Newton's Laws; quadratic equations applied to Matrix Operations).
3. **Misconception Correction Incoherence**: While the model correctly identified the stated misconception, its refutation was logically incoherent, asserting that a misconception in one area was disproven by the definition of an adjacent, unrelated concept (e.g., refuting differentiability misconceptions with linear algebra definitions).
4. **Document Grounding Failure**: All 20 benchmark responses scored $\le 1$ on document grounding (mean 0.95), with zero instances of retrieved evidence cited or utilized.

### Primary Audit Finding

These failures are **not** caused by model capacity limitations, LoRA rank constraints, or learning degradation. They are directly traceable to **five concrete structural defects in the pipeline and dataset generation**:

| Defect ID | Defect Classification | Location | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D-1** | **Prompt Formatting Erasure** | [`src/oloric/formatting.py:37-50`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/src/oloric/formatting.py#L37-L50) | `_format_chatml()` erases `model_input.task` and `instruction` from the prompt. The model was trained and evaluated without ever seeing the instruction! |
| **D-2** | **Evaluation Input Truncation** | [`src/oloric/evaluation.py:86`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/src/oloric/evaluation.py#L86) | `evaluate_example()` passes `example["context"]` into `OloricModelInput`, dropping root-level `example["instruction"]`. |

| **D-4** | **Domain-Level Fixed Example Pools** | [`scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py:11-18, 63-70`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py#L11-L18) | Exactly **one** hardcoded numerical example pair and **one** concrete example pair per domain. In `evaluation/oloric-v0.2/responses.jsonl`, 13 of 20 benchmark responses emitted `target.strategy = "concrete_example"` and `target.action = "explain"`. The remaining 7 responses split into: `misconception_correction` (2), `diagnostic_questions` (1), `practice` (1), `prerequisite` (1), `hint` (1), and `analogy` (1). Zero responses selected `follow_up_questions`.

```

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
follow_up_questions                5     concrete_example (3), analogy (1), diagnostic_questions (1)
diagnostic_questions               4     concrete_example (3), concrete_example [numerical] (1)
```

Every single failure occurred on examples requesting **question generation** or **explanation** (`follow_up_questions`, `diagnostic_questions`, `simple_explanation`). Conversely, `hint_based_teaching`, `practice_questions`, `prerequisite_detection`, and `misconception_detection` **successfully selected their respective strategies**.

### 2.2 Root Cause 1: Instruction Dropped by ChatML Formatter (Code Bug)

In [`src/oloric/formatting.py:37-50`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/src/oloric/formatting.py#L37-L50):

```python
def _format_chatml(self, model_input: OloricModelInput) -> str:
    messages = []
    system_content = self._build_system_message(model_input)
    messages.append({"role": "system", "content": system_content})
    for turn in model_input.conversation_context:
        role = "user" if turn.role == "student" else ("assistant" if turn.role in ("tutor", "model") else turn.role)
        messages.append({"role": role, "content": turn.content})
    try:
        formatted = self.tokenizer.apply_chat_template(
            messages, tokenize=False, add_generation_prompt=True
        )
    except Exception:
        formatted = self._format_simple(model_input)
    return formatted
```

Now inspect `_build_system_message(self, model_input: OloricModelInput)` in [`src/oloric/formatting.py:103-141`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/src/oloric/formatting.py#L103-L141):
`_build_system_message` formats `doc_ctx`, `learner_state`, and `current_goal`. **It never mentions `model_input.task`**.

And in `_format_chatml`:
- In `_format_alpaca`, the code explicitly adds: `### Instruction:\n{model_input.task}\n\n`
- In `_format_simple`, the code explicitly adds: `Task: {model_input.task}\n\n`
- In `_format_chatml` (the active template configured in [`configs/dataset.yaml:66`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/configs/dataset.yaml#L66)), **`model_input.task` is completely omitted from the message list!**

### 2.3 Root Cause 2: Evaluation Runner Omits Root `instruction` Field

In [`src/oloric/evaluation.py:86`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/src/oloric/evaluation.py#L86):

```python
# Convert context and target to proper objects
context = OloricModelInput(**example["context"])
```

In `evaluation/benchmark.jsonl`, the natural language instruction (e.g., `"Ask diagnostic questions to identify the nature of confusion about MPC"`) resides at the root level: `example["instruction"]`.
`example["context"]` contains:
```json
"context": {
  "task": "resolve_confusion",
  ...
}
```
Thus, even if `_format_chatml` had checked `model_input.task`, it would have received `"resolve_confusion"`, not `"Ask diagnostic questions..."`.

### 2.4 Root Cause 3: Input Indistinguishability in Dataset Construction

Why did `practice_questions`, `hint_based_teaching`, `prerequisite_detection`, and `misconception_detection` succeed in strategy selection, while `diagnostic_questions` and `follow_up_questions` collapsed?

Look at the student conversation turns in the benchmark:
- `hint_based_teaching`: student says `"Can you give me a hint?"` $\rightarrow$ model outputs `strategy = "hint"`!
- `misconception_detection`: student says `"I think All continuous functions are differentiable."` $\rightarrow$ model outputs `strategy = "misconception_correction"`!
- `practice_questions`: student says `"I'd like to practice Effective Communication..."` $\rightarrow$ model outputs `strategy = "practice"`!
- `prerequisite_detection`: student says `"But I don't understand percentages. How is that related?"` $\rightarrow$ model outputs `strategy = "prerequisite"`!

Now look at the remaining **15 benchmark examples**:
All 15 contain the identical turn:
`[{"role": "student", "content": "I'm struggling to understand {concept}."}]`

And in the training dataset (`data/generated/oloric_v02_dataset.jsonl`), **192 records across 8 distinct categories** have that exact same turn:
- `concrete_example` (24 records)
- `numerical_example` (24 records)
- `diagnostic_questions` (24 records)
- `follow_up_questions` (24 records)
- `simple_explanation` (24 records)
- `simplification` (24 records)
- `analogy` (24 records)
- `error_correction` (24 records)

Because `instruction` was stripped by `_format_chatml`, **the input prompt to the model was 100% identical across all 192 examples**:
```html
<|im_start|>system
You are OLORIC... Document Context... Learner State... Current Goal: Understand {concept}...<|im_end|>
<|im_start|>user
I'm struggling to understand {concept}.<|im_end|>
<|im_start|>assistant
```
The model was asked to learn a 1-to-8 multimodal mapping from an identical prompt. In that competition:
- `concrete_example` (24) + `numerical_example` (24, which also specifies `strategy: "concrete_example"`) = 48 records supervising `strategy = "concrete_example"`.
- This represents twice the frequency of any other single-turn category, causing the model to collapse to `strategy: "concrete_example"`.

### 3.3 Root Cause 2: Arbitrary Hash-Modulo Fallback in `fix_seed_data_v02.py`

In [`scripts/fix_seed_data_v02.py:313-316`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/scripts/fix_seed_data_v02.py#L313-L316):

```python
def det_get_concept_example(domain, concept):
    pairs = gi.DOMAIN_CONTENT[domain]["examples"]
    idx = sum(ord(ch) for ch in concept) % len(pairs)
    return pairs[idx][0]
```

`DOMAIN_CONTENT[domain]["examples"]` contains a list of 10 generic examples for the domain. Rather than matching by concept name or semantic relevance, the code hashes character ordinal sums:
- `Enzyme Activity`: `sum(ord) % 10 = 9` $\rightarrow$ `"Soluble fiber dissolves in water and can help lower cholesterol"`
- `Food Preservation`: `sum(ord) % 10 = 6` $\rightarrow$ `"The sum of all chemical reactions in a living organism is metabolism"`

In `v02_nutrition_food_science_multi_turn_tutoring_003`, the target response is:
`"Great question! digestion and every biochemical reaction... works by the rate at which enzymes catalyze reactions... To see this in practice, Soluble fiber dissolves in water and can help lower cholesterol."`

The dataset explicitly supervised the model to connect Enzyme Activity to soluble fiber, and Food Preservation to metabolism. The model did not hallucinate these connections; it reproduced the supervised targets with high fidelity.

## 4. Misconception Correction Root Cause

### 4.1 Phenomenon in Benchmark Evaluation

In `evaluation/oloric-v0.2/responses.jsonl`:
- `mathematics_misconception_detection_012` (Concept: `Linear Algebra`):
  `"A common misconception is that 'All continuous functions are differentiable'. This is incorrect because the study of vectors, matrices, and linear transformations, central to solving systems of equations shows that it provides tools for solving problems and understanding patterns. The correct understanding is that the study of vectors, matrices, and linear transformations... actually means it provides tools for solving problems and understanding patterns."`
- `general_academic_misconception_detection_008` (Concept: `Learning Strategies`):
  `"A common misconception is that 'Experts are never biased'. This is incorrect because techniques like spaced repetition and active recall that improve retention and understanding shows that it underpins rigorous academic work and rational thought. The correct understanding is that techniques like spaced repetition and active recall... actually means it underpins rigorous academic work and rational thought."`

### 4.2 Root Cause 1: Independent Cartesian Product Sampling

In [`scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py:281-285`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py#L281-L285):

```python
def create_misconception_correction(domain: str, index: int) -> Dict[str, Any]:
    domain_info = DOMAIN_CONTENT[domain]
    concept = random.choice(domain_info["concepts"])
    misconception = random.choice(domain_info["misconceptions"])
```

`concept` and `misconception` are drawn independently from domain-wide lists.
- In `mathematics`, `misconceptions` contains calculus, algebra, and statistics items (`"All continuous functions are differentiable"`, `"√(a² + b²) = a + b"`, `"The median is always between the min and max"`).
- `concepts` contains `Linear Algebra`, `Quadratic Equations`, `Probability Distributions`, etc.
Because there is no concept-to-misconception mapping table, a calculus misconception is assigned to Linear Algebra, an expert bias misconception is assigned to Learning Strategies, and an electric current misconception is assigned to Chemical Bonding.

### 4.3 Root Cause 2: Syntactically Formulaic Non-Sequitur Template

In [`scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py:304-308`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py#L304-L308):

```python
"response": f"A common misconception is that '{misconception}'. This is incorrect because {get_concept_explanation(domain, concept)} shows that {get_concept_reason(domain, concept)}. The correct understanding is that {get_concept_explanation(domain, concept)} actually means {get_concept_reason(domain, concept)}."
```

This f-string template asserts:
$$\text{Misconception } M \text{ is false because } \text{Definition of } C \text{ demonstrates that } \text{Reason for } C.$$
There is no counterexample, no causal argument, and no logical refutation. The generator simply glues the definition of the concept into the slot where a logical disproof should be.

### 4.4 Dataset Quantification

- **Affected records in v0.2**: All 24 records in `misconception_detection` (100%) and all 24 records in `error_correction` (100%) use this exact structure (total: 48 records = 10% of dataset).
- **Split distribution**: Train: 39, Validation: 3, Test: 6.
- The model memorized this template and reproduced it down to the exact phrases `"shows that it provides tools for solving problems"` and `"actually means it underpins rigorous academic work"`.

## 5. Document Grounding Root Cause

### 5.1 Phenomenon in Benchmark Evaluation

In `evaluation/oloric-v0.2/scores_semantic.jsonl`, all 20 benchmark responses scored $\le 1$ on Document Grounding (19 scored 1, 1 scored 0). Not a single response cited the document title, page number, section, or retrieved evidence.

### 5.2 Root Cause 1: Zero Benchmark Examples for Document Grounding

`evaluation/benchmark.jsonl` contains 20 items. Its category breakdown is:
`follow_up_questions` (5), `diagnostic_questions` (4), `numerical_example` (4), `misconception_detection` (2), `hint_based_teaching` (1), `concrete_example` (1), `simple_explanation` (1), `practice_questions` (1), `prerequisite_detection` (1).
**Category `document_grounded` is 0/20 in the benchmark.**

### 5.3 Root Cause 2: Strategy-Label Mismatch in Generator

In [`scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py:368-369`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py#L368-L369):

```python
"target": {
    "action": "explain",
    "strategy": "simple_explanation",  # <-- MISLABEL! Should be document_grounded
```

Even for the 24 `document_grounded` training examples, the target strategy is labeled `simple_explanation`. Across all 480 training records, **the strategy string `"document_grounded"` appears exactly 0 times**. The model was never supervised to predict `strategy: "document_grounded"`.

### 5.4 Root Cause 3: Hallucinatory Grounding Supervision in Targets

In [`scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py:371-375`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py#L371-L375):

```python
"response": f"Looking at our document, we can see that {concept} is clearly explained in the selected text: '{get_concept_explanation(domain, concept)}'. The surrounding context tells us that {get_concept_reason(domain, concept)}. The retrieved evidence supports this by showing {get_concept_example(domain, concept)}."
```

Now compare this to what `create_base_context` puts in the input context ([`scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py:233-235`](file:///c:/oloric%20v01/oloric-model/scripts/generate_seed_data_improved.py#L233-L235)):
- `context.document_context.selected_text` = `"The concept of {concept} is fundamental to understanding {domain}..."`
- `context.document_context.retrieved_evidence` = random sample of formulas (e.g., `["F = ma", "E = mc²"]`).

**Target/Input Contradiction**:
1. The target response claims: `"clearly explained in the selected text: '{get_concept_explanation}'"`, but `selected_text` in the prompt contains `"The concept of {concept} is fundamental..."`.
2. The target response claims: `"The retrieved evidence supports this by showing {get_concept_example}"`, but `retrieved_evidence` in the prompt contains mathematical formulas, not `get_concept_example`!

The training targets taught the model to ignore what is actually inside `selected_text` and `retrieved_evidence`, and instead substitute generic parametric definitions.

### 5.5 Root Cause 4: Document Context Omission in 95% of Records

In 456 out of 480 training records (all categories except `document_grounded`), the target response body makes **zero reference** to the document context or retrieved evidence. The model correctly learned that in 95% of tutoring interactions, document context is unreferenced metadata.
